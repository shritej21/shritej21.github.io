About Me

Who I Am

I'm an AI/ML Engineer with 3.5 years of experience shipping machine learning and Generative AI systems into production across financial services and business applications. Today I work at Visa, building LLM-powered tooling for payment operations — copilots, agentic workflows, and retrieval systems that analysts depend on every day.

My core stack is Python, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, LangChain, LangGraph, and AWS. I've built everything from XGBoost fraud and credit-risk models to hybrid-search RAG pipelines with query rewriting, reranking, and graph-based retrieval — plus the evaluation harnesses that decide whether any of it is actually good enough to release.

I care most about the unglamorous half of AI engineering: groundedness checks, LLM-as-a-judge scoring, regression testing, PII redaction, prompt-injection defenses, drift monitoring. Models are easy to demo and hard to trust — I like working on the trust part.
